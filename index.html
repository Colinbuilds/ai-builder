How it works

From the supply counter to the customer's paperwork

  1. Crew sends it from the counter

    On their phone: pick the job, take a photo of the receipt, tap send. Add a note like "rotted decking, customer approved" if it's extra work.

  2. AI reads every line

    Vendor, date, items, quantities, tax and total. It checks the math and matches the job to your Drive folder by name or address.

  3. Office sets the price

    Markup starts at 15% and can be changed on any receipt. You see cost, billed total, profit and margin before anything goes out.

  4. One click makes the paperwork

    A change order from your own template or an invoice, saved in the job's folder and named after it. QuickBooks Online gets the estimate or invoice.

Who sees what

Your crew sends receipts. Your office sees the money.

Crew · phone
  • Sends receipts from the supply counter in a few taps
  • Sees only the receipts they sent, and whether each is approved
  • Never sees cost, markup or profit
Office · admin
  • Sees every receipt from every employee
  • Fixes the job, items or tax, and sets the markup
  • Chooses change order or invoice and approves
  • Totals for each job: cost, billed, profit and what's still pending

Built for real receipts

It handles the paperwork crews actually bring back

Your job names, as they are in Drive

Change orders and invoices are named after the job folder, like "Change Order #3 - Dana Whitfield - 4410 Maple Ridge Dr Omaha NE".

Unclear jobs get a question, not a guess

A receipt that just says "Northgate" when you have Northgate Commons and Northgate Plaza goes to the office to pick.

Delivery tickets with no prices

Pickup tickets list quantities but no prices. They're held for the office to type in costs, never billed at $0.

No prices on this receipt. Type in what each line cost before approving.

Math that doesn't add up is flagged

If the lines don't match the subtotal, or the subtotal plus tax doesn't match the total, the office sees it before approving.

Your own change order template

Fills in project name, location, date, description, reason, net change and new contract total, and adds the materials table above the signatures.

Next change order number, automatically

It reads the job's Change Orders folder and uses the next number, so CO #2 is followed by CO #3.

Where it runs

Inside your Google Workspace, connected to QuickBooks Online

Google Drive and Docs

Receipts are filed in each job's Receipts folder. Change orders and invoices are saved as a Google Doc and a PDF in the job's folders. Your crew signs in with their company Google account.

QuickBooks Online

Change orders go in as estimates and invoices as invoices, under the customer named like your job folder. Material costs can be recorded as expenses on the job.

A record of every receipt

Every receipt, who sent it, what it cost and what was billed is kept in a Google Sheet in your Drive.
