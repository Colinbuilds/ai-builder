Questions

Do I need to know anything about AI?

No. Each tool asks a few simple questions, and the AI instructions are built in. You never write a prompt.

What can the app see in my Google Drive?

Only the files it creates. It uses Google's most limited Drive permission, so it can't open or read anything else in your Drive. Your results go into a folder called “AI Business Toolkit”.

Which AI does it use?

Claude, by Anthropic. You don't need your own account or API key; it's included in your subscription.

What counts as an AI run?

Each time you click “Generate with AI” counts as one run. Saving to Drive and downloading CSVs are free. Runs reset on the 1st of every month.

Can I use the results in Excel?

Yes. Download any result as a CSV file that opens in Excel, or open the Google Sheet and choose File → Download → Microsoft Excel.

How do I cancel?

Click your account icon in the app and choose “Manage subscription”. You can cancel in two clicks and keep access until the end of your billing period.

Is the bookkeeping output tax advice?

No. It organizes and categorizes your transactions and flags items to check, but have a qualified accountant review your taxes.
