What we collect

  • Your Google account email, to identify you and match you to your subscription.
  • Billing details, collected and stored by Stripe, our payment processor. We never see your full card number.
  • What you type into the tools, which is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate your results. We don't store it on our servers.
  • A monthly usage count, stored with your Stripe customer record to enforce plan limits.

Google user data

The app asks for permission to see your email address and to create files in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). With it, the app can only create and access the files it makes for you: spreadsheets in a folder called "AI Business Toolkit". It can't see or open any other file in your Drive. Files are created directly from your browser; your Drive contents never pass through our servers.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don't sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Who we share data with

Stripe (payments), Anthropic (AI processing), Google (sign-in and Drive) and Cloudflare (hosting our API). We don't sell your data.

Your choices

You can revoke the app's access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and cancel your subscription from the app. Email us to delete your account data.
