## Decision

**The AI Solo Business Kit** sits where the three strongest signals overlap:

1. **Business & Money** is the #2 real category on Gumroad and earns about $10k per product.
2. **AI prompt packs** are the fastest-growing product type.
3. **Templates and trackers** carry the highest margins and sell the most units on Etsy.

A bundle beats a single prompt pack. Prompt lists alone have become commoditized, and pairing them with working spreadsheets makes a "done-for-you kit", which is itself one of the fast-growing formats listed above. It also allows a higher price than a $9 prompt PDF.

**Target buyer:** freelancers, solopreneurs and side-hustlers who aren't technical and want AI to run their marketing, sales, operations and finances.

**Pricing** (from the price bands above):
- Starter: $19 (prompt pack PDF only)
- **Complete Kit: $49** (prompts, 3 spreadsheets and the playbook), the anchor tier
- Pro: $97 (Complete Kit plus editable source files and a commercial/agency licence)
